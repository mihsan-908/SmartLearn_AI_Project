About SmartLearn AI

SmartLearn AI is an intelligent study assistant engineered to help students, educators, and professionals understand complex study materials faster.

Mission

To democratize high-quality, personalized learning by turning documents and lectures into interactive, digestible knowledge.

How it Works

Upload your PDF notes or textbooks. Our system extracts the key content and uses state-of-the-art language models to answer questions, generate summaries, produce test quizzes, and create revision notes.

Privacy & Security

Your documents and interactions are securely handled and kept private to your account.
